# Demo recording script (target 2:30, hard limit 3:00)

The rules ask for a video that shows the project **working on its platform**. The 40-second animated video (`brag-output/brag.mp4`) is good as an opening, but it is screenshots, not the app running. So the entry video is: the first 20 seconds of the animated video, then your own screen recording of the live app. Send me the recording and I will join the two, add the music credit and check the length.

## Before you record
1. Close other heavy programs. Plug in the laptop. A busy machine made the scan take over 3 minutes in tests.
2. Open desktop Chrome, go to https://eleven-months-nano.web.app, press **Load sample** and **Scan lease** once, and wait for it to finish. This loads the model. Then reload the page.
3. Have a phone photo of a printed page of English text ready on the laptop (or print the sample and photograph it).
4. Set the browser zoom to 110% so text is readable in the video. Hide bookmarks and other tabs.
5. Record with Cmd+Shift+5, "Record Selected Portion", around the browser window, microphone on.
6. The scan has slow stretches. Keep recording; I will cut the waiting out. Say each line only when the screen shows it.
