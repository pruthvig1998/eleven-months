Read the fine print before it reads you.

Eleven Months checks your rental agreement clause by clause, tells you which ones can cost you, and what to ask the landlord for instead. It reads on your device. Built for Telangana tenants.

Check my agreement Free. Paste text, a PDF or a photo.

Why the name? Most rental agreements in India run 11 months, so they don't have to be registered. Most tenants sign one without anyone reading it for them. Why 11 months

Your agreement stays with you

Each clause takes the shortest route that can still judge it.

  1. Fixed rules

    Read every number: deposit, notice, lock-in, rent rise. Rules always win on figures.

  2. On your device

    Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano judges each clause. Nothing leaves your laptop.

  3. Cloud, masked

    Only when the two disagree. Names, Aadhaar, PAN, phones and emails are masked first.
